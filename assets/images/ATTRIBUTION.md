# Demonstration Image Sources & Attribution

All product demonstration images stored in this directory (`assets/images/products/`) are downloaded locally to ensure the store never depends on external hotlinks, third-party stock-image CDN availability, or remote URL changes.

These images serve as realistic demonstration placeholders. Store owners can upload genuine photographs taken from their smartphone cameras directly via the Admin Dashboard (`/admin/`).

## Replacing Demonstration Images With Live Product Photos

1. Log into the Owner Dashboard at `/admin/`.
2. Tap **Add Product** or tap **Edit** on any existing product listing.
3. Tap **Upload Photographs** and choose files straight from your smartphone photo gallery or take a fresh photo with the camera.
4. The photographs are uploaded to the Supabase Storage bucket (`product-images`) and associated with the device listing immediately.
